## 从仓库到生态

每条知识都应该带着来源、时间、确信程度和它影响过的其他想法。这样，AI 才能协助整理，而不是把推测包装成事实。

这篇文章会连接 [[Agent 记忆的三层结构]] 与 [[知识萌发]]，逐步形成一套适合个人研究和创作的工作流。

<IntroParagraph>A knowledge base stores answers. A knowledge garden records how ideas change. AI should not merely help us hoard more material; it should help old and new ideas form meaningful relationships.</IntroParagraph>

## From repository to ecosystem

Each piece of knowledge should carry its sources, time, confidence, and the ideas it has influenced. That lets AI help organise knowledge without disguising inference as fact.

This essay connects [the three layers of agent memory](/agent-memory-layers) with [knowledge germination](/knowledge-germination) to develop a workflow for personal research and making.
