<IntroParagraph>可信并不意味着 AI 永远正确，而是人在它不确定、出错或越界时，依然知道发生了什么并能接管。</IntroParagraph>

## 四种需要可见的信息

1. 结论依据了哪些来源。
2. 哪些内容是事实，哪些是推断。
3. 系统已经执行了什么动作。
4. 用户如何撤销、修正或追溯。

后续会把这些原则整理成 [[可见的来源链]] 设计模式，并补充不同风险等级下的信息密度。
</LanguageBlock>

<LanguageBlock lang="en">
<IntroParagraph>Trust does not mean AI is always right. It means that when the system is uncertain, wrong, or out of bounds, people can still understand what happened and take control.</IntroParagraph>

## Four things that should remain visible

1. Which sources support the conclusion.
2. What is fact and what is inference.
3. Which actions the system has already taken.
4. How a person can undo, correct, or trace the result.

These principles will grow into the [visible provenance](/visible-provenance) pattern, including how information density should change with risk.
</LanguageBlock>
