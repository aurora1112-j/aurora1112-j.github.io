## 核心问题

一个可信的 Agent 应该能区分三种动作：可以直接完成的可逆动作、需要解释后执行的高影响动作，以及必须由人确认的不可逆动作。

这篇文章将进一步讨论 [[渐进式自主权]]、失败后的恢复路径，以及为什么“少打扰”不等于“少让用户知道”。

## The central question

A trustworthy agent should distinguish reversible actions it may complete, consequential actions it should explain first, and irreversible actions that require explicit human approval.

This essay will examine [progressive autonomy](/progressive-autonomy), recovery after failure, and why fewer interruptions should never mean less awareness.
