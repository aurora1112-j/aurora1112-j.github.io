<LanguageBlock lang="zh">

Botanical Systems 把知识理解为持续变化的生态，而不是静态文件的仓库。

植物意象在这里不是装饰：根代表来源，枝代表关系，年轮代表修订，树冠代表可以被他人理解和复用的成熟知识。
</LanguageBlock>

<LanguageBlock lang="en">
Botanical Systems treats knowledge as a changing ecology rather than a warehouse of static files.

The plant language is not decoration: roots stand for provenance, branches for relationships, rings for revision, and the canopy for mature knowledge others can understand and reuse.
</LanguageBlock>
