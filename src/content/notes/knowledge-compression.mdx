<LanguageBlock lang="zh">

AI 摘要通常把多条证据压缩成一个顺滑结论。被压掉的往往不是废话，而是作者之间的分歧、研究条件、反例和不确定性。

一个更适合知识工作的摘要，应该允许读者展开查看：原始来源、不同观点、被省略的限定条件，以及系统自己的确信程度。

待验证：把同一组材料分别压缩为“一句话结论”和“可展开证据树”，比较理解与纠错效率。
</LanguageBlock>

<LanguageBlock lang="en">
AI summaries often compress many pieces of evidence into one smooth conclusion. What disappears is not merely noise, but disagreement, research conditions, counterexamples, and uncertainty.

A better summary for knowledge work should let readers expand the original sources, conflicting views, omitted qualifications, and the system's confidence.

To test: compress the same material into a one-line conclusion and an expandable evidence tree, then compare understanding and correction.
</LanguageBlock>
