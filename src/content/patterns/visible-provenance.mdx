## 情境

一个流畅答案可能混合事实、推断和过时信息，用户却只能看到最终文本。

## 模式

在结论旁显示轻量来源标记。展开后呈现原始材料、提取时间、推断关系和修订记录。来源链不应该成为页面底部的一堆链接，而要与具体主张对齐。

这个模式与 [[可信 AI 系统需要展示什么]] 共同生长。
</LanguageBlock>

<LanguageBlock lang="en">
## Context

A fluent answer may blend facts, inference, and outdated information while revealing only the final prose.

## Pattern

Place a lightweight source marker beside each claim. Expand it to reveal the original material, retrieval time, inference relationship, and revision history. Provenance should align with specific claims, not become a pile of links at the bottom.

This pattern grows alongside [what trustworthy AI systems need to show](/trustworthy-ai-systems).
</LanguageBlock>
