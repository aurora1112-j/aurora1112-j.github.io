## 情境

完全自动会让人失去控制，每一步确认又会让 Agent 退化成笨重的表单。

## 模式

按照动作的可逆性与影响范围分层授权：低风险动作直接执行并留下记录；中风险动作先展示计划；高风险或不可逆动作必须明确确认。

授权可以被用户调整，也应该在失败、环境变化或连续不确定时自动收紧。
</LanguageBlock>

<LanguageBlock lang="en">
## Context

Full automation removes control, while asking for confirmation at every step turns an agent into a cumbersome form.

## Pattern

Layer authorization by reversibility and impact. Execute low-risk actions and leave a record; show a plan before medium-risk actions; require explicit approval for high-risk or irreversible actions.

People can tune the boundary, and the system should narrow it automatically after failure, environmental change, or sustained uncertainty.
</LanguageBlock>
