## 情境

收藏夹把刚看到的链接与已经验证的认识放在同一个平面上，数量增长却没有成熟过程。

## 模式

让内容经历“种子—扎根—分枝—树冠”四个阶段。阶段不是完成度评分，而是在提示当前内容的证据、连接与可复用程度。

AI 可以建议连接和缺失证据，但成熟状态最终由作者确认。
</LanguageBlock>

<LanguageBlock lang="en">
## Context

Bookmarks place a link seen moments ago on the same plane as a tested belief. The collection grows, but nothing matures.

## Pattern

Let content move through four stages: seed, roots, branches, and canopy. A stage is not a completion score; it signals the evidence, connections, and reusability a piece currently has.

AI may suggest connections and missing evidence, but the author confirms maturity.
</LanguageBlock>
