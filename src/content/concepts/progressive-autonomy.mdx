<IntroParagraph>自主权不是一个开关，而是一组可以按动作、场景和信任水平调整的权限。</IntroParagraph>

## 三层行动

1. 可逆、低风险动作可以直接完成并留下记录。
2. 高影响但可恢复的动作应在执行前解释计划。
3. 不可逆或涉及他人的动作必须明确确认。

## 设计目标

减少不必要的打断，同时让用户始终知道系统做过什么、接下来准备做什么，以及如何撤销。

## 执行成本下降后的责任

Agent 可以让执行越来越便宜，但不会自动回答“什么值得做”。自主权设计最终仍要让判断者和责任归属保持清晰。参见碎片：[执行变便宜后，判断更重要](/initiative-after-execution-gets-cheap)。
</LanguageBlock>

<LanguageBlock lang="en">

<IntroParagraph>Autonomy is not a switch. It is a set of permissions that can vary by action, situation, and level of trust.</IntroParagraph>

## Three action layers

1. Reversible, low-risk actions can complete directly with a visible record.
2. Consequential but recoverable actions should explain the plan first.
3. Irreversible actions or actions affecting others require explicit confirmation.

## Design goal

Reduce needless interruption while keeping past actions, future intent, and reversal paths legible.

## Responsibility after execution gets cheaper

Agents can keep lowering the cost of execution, but they do not automatically answer what is worth doing. Autonomy design must keep judgement and responsibility legible. See the fragment: [When Execution Gets Cheaper, Judgement Matters More](/initiative-after-execution-gets-cheap).
</LanguageBlock>
