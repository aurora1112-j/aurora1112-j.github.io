<IntroParagraph>KnoTree 把研究过程从“得到一份答案”改写为“培育一棵树”：AI 提供分支，用户决定哪些值得继续生长。</IntroParagraph>

## 交互模型

1. 从一个主题种子开始
2. AI 把主题拆成可以探索的分支
3. 搜索资源以叶片形式附着在分支上
4. 用户修剪无关路径，保留真正有价值的脉络
5. 导出经过选择的知识路径，而不是未经整理的答案堆

## 为什么它属于 Botanical Systems

KnoTree 的“生长”不是装饰性的比喻。分支、叶片和修剪分别对应问题拆解、证据补充和主动取舍，人的判断始终留在回路中。

项目采用 React、React Flow、Zustand 与 Express，并通过 Tavily 补充外部资料。

[查看 GitHub 项目](https://github.com/aurora1112-j/knotree)

<IntroParagraph>KnoTree reframes research from “receiving an answer” to “cultivating a tree”: AI proposes branches, while the user decides what deserves to grow.</IntroParagraph>

## Interaction model

1. Begin with a topic seed
2. Let AI decompose it into explorable branches
3. Attach searched resources as leaves
4. Prune irrelevant paths and preserve useful lines of inquiry
5. Export a curated knowledge path rather than an unedited answer pile

## Why it belongs in Botanical Systems

Growth is not merely decorative here. Branching, leafing, and pruning correspond to decomposition, evidence gathering, and deliberate selection, keeping human judgement in the loop.

The project uses React, React Flow, Zustand, and Express, with Tavily for external resources.

[View the project on GitHub](https://github.com/aurora1112-j/knotree)
