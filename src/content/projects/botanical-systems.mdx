## 问题

我一直在思考作品集、个人网站应该怎么收录思考的碎片与完成的结果，但是传统作品集很容易变成按时间堆积的文档，缺少一些思维本身的活跃度和发散力。因此这个项目希望同时保留来源、关系、成熟度与修订过程，我把它们叫做根、芽、分枝、年轮、树冠，就像经营一座花园。

## 思想起点

聊天和信息流不断用新的内容覆盖旧内容，而花园允许想法累积、分枝和修订。这个差异构成了项目的起点。参见碎片：[花园是为积累而设计的](/gardens-are-built-to-accumulate)。

## Problem

I have kept wondering how a portfolio or personal site can hold both fragments of thought and finished work. Traditional portfolios easily turn into chronological stacks of documents, with little room for ideas to stay active or branch out. Botanical Systems keeps provenance, relationships, maturity, and revision visible. I call them roots, buds, branches, rings, and canopy. It is closer to tending a garden.

## Point of departure

Chats and feeds continually cover older material with something new; a garden lets ideas accumulate, branch, and change. That difference became the project's point of departure. See the fragment: [A Garden Is Built to Accumulate](/gardens-are-built-to-accumulate).

## What it looks like now

- A bilingual personal site
- Fragments and short reflections, collected concepts, finished essays, and photographic records
- Ways to wander by form and by when something began to grow
- Cards that link to one another
