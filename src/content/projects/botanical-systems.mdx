## 问题

传统作品集倾向展示完成结果，知识库则容易变成按时间堆积的文档。这个项目希望同时保留来源、关系、成熟度与修订过程。

## 思想起点

聊天和信息流不断用新的内容覆盖旧内容，而花园允许想法累积、分枝和修订。这个差异构成了项目的起点。参见碎片：[花园是为积累而设计的](/gardens-are-built-to-accumulate)。

## 当前形态

- 中英文个人网站
- 概念、作品、摄影和碎片四类内容
- 可筛选的统一花园
- 可追溯的内容版本与关联

## 下一步

接入真实项目、摄影系列与个人知识工作流。

<LanguageBlock lang="en">

<IntroParagraph>Botanical Systems is not an imitation of plant aesthetics. It uses roots, buds, branches, rings, grafts, and canopy to describe how knowledge forms.</IntroParagraph>

## Problem

Portfolios favour finished outcomes, while knowledge bases often become chronological piles of documents. This project keeps provenance, relationships, maturity, and revision visible together.

## Point of departure

Chats and feeds continually cover older material with something new; a garden lets ideas accumulate, branch, and change. That difference became the project's point of departure. See the fragment: [A Garden Is Built to Accumulate](/gardens-are-built-to-accumulate).

## Current form

- A bilingual personal site
- Concepts, works, photography, and fragments
- One filterable garden
- Traceable content versions and relationships

## Next

Connect real projects, photographic series, and a personal knowledge workflow.
