<IntroParagraph>这个设计系统从一个问题开始：怎样让 AI 辅助思考这种看不见的过程，变得可见、可导航，也更值得信任？</IntroParagraph>

## 一个系统，而不是几套物料

Vesti 的产品界面、海报、明信片和书签不被当作彼此分离的产物。它们共同解释四件事：记忆如何被捕获、知识如何被检索、想法如何形成网络，以及本地优先如何保护用户控制权。

## 海报如何进入产品

海报不是被放进“品牌档案”后搁置，而是被拆成可执行的规则：温暖的纸张是默认场地，墨色负责信息层级，细线轨迹表达思维之间的路径。仓库中的正式猫头鹰图标负责产品识别；海报里由微小符号组成的 8 字回环负责表达记忆不断返回、连接和生长。两者是不同来源的核心资产，不把底纹重新画进猫头鹰身体。

产品代码仍然是功能与状态的事实来源。Light / Dark 语义变量、八个平台身份、捕获与检索过程、错误和来源信息都来自真实界面；海报负责身份，代码负责行为，设计系统负责在两者之间翻译。

## 信任如何进入视觉

本地存储、零云端默认值与可迁移入口被反复放在显眼位置。隐私不是藏在说明页里的承诺，而是每个媒介都需要持续表达的产品能力。

## 跨媒介的连续性

界面负责产品逻辑，海报负责在更远距离传递完整命题，明信片和书签则把系统压缩到可以带走的尺度。成功的标准不是每件物料都相似，而是用户在阅读规格之前就能感到它们属于同一种产品判断。

## 反思

早期版本更强调品牌存在感，后续迭代逐步把功能分类与产品逻辑带到前景。最重要的转变，是让印刷语言成为界面的延伸，而不是产品之外的一层宣传包装。
</LanguageBlock>

<IntroParagraph>This design system began with one question: how can an invisible process such as AI-assisted thinking become visible, navigable, and trustworthy?</IntroParagraph>

## One system, not separate deliverables

The Vesti interface, posters, postcard, and bookmark are treated as one family. Together they explain how memory is captured, knowledge retrieved, thought networked, and local-first architecture translated into user control.

## How the specification is formed

The poster is not filed away as a moodboard. It is decomposed into executable rules: warm paper as the default ground, ink as hierarchy, and hairline trajectories as paths between ideas. The repository's official owl identifies the product; the poster's glyph-filled figure eight expresses memory returning, connecting, and growing. They are distinct source assets, and the texture is not redrawn inside the owl.

Product code remains authoritative for function and state. Light / Dark semantic variables, eight platform identities, capture and retrieval stages, errors, and source evidence come from the real interface. The poster supplies identity, code supplies behavior, and the design system translates between them.

## Making trust visible

Local storage, zero-cloud defaults, and portability remain prominent across surfaces. Privacy is not a promise hidden in documentation; it is a product capability every medium must continue to communicate.
</LanguageBlock>

<LanguageBlock lang="en">

## Continuity across media

The interface carries product logic, posters communicate the complete proposition at distance, and postcards and bookmarks compress the system to a portable scale. Success does not mean every artifact looks identical; it means they feel driven by the same product judgement before a specification is read.

## Reflection

Earlier iterations foregrounded brand presence. Later work brought feature taxonomy and product logic forward, allowing print to feel like an extension of the interface rather than promotional packaging around it.
</LanguageBlock>
