<IntroParagraph>这个 Skill 用视觉隐喻、机制图与带注释的构图解释复杂观点，让插图承担推理工作，而不是成为文章旁边的装饰。</IntroParagraph>

## 它会做什么

- 根据论点选择概念插画、科学解释图、机制图或封面构图
- 把来源、因果、层级和时间变化组织进同一幅图
- 生成可用于视觉文章、演示或知识笔记的原创图像
- 把审美参考转译成通用视觉属性，不模仿在世艺术家的风格

## 原创性原则

它不抓取网络素材，也不复刻指定艺术家。参考只被拆解为线条、材质、留白、色彩与信息密度等可描述属性，再根据当前主题重新构图。

## 与设计系统的关系

相比固定组件库，它更像一套“视觉语法”：保留一致的手工感与解释密度，同时让每一幅图根据内容自然长出不同结构。

<IntroParagraph>This skill uses visual metaphors, mechanism diagrams, and annotated compositions to explain difficult ideas, making illustration part of the reasoning rather than decoration beside it.</IntroParagraph>

## What it does

- Selects conceptual illustration, scientific explainer, mechanism diagram, or cover composition according to the argument
- Organises provenance, causality, hierarchy, and change over time in one image
- Produces original visuals for essays, presentations, and knowledge notes
- Translates aesthetic references into general visual properties without imitating living artists

## Originality principle

It neither scrapes web assets nor reproduces a named artist. References are decomposed into describable properties—line, texture, whitespace, colour, and information density—then recomposed for the subject at hand.

## Relation to the design system

Rather than a fixed component library, it behaves like a visual grammar: preserving a handmade explanatory character while allowing each image to grow a structure suited to its content.
