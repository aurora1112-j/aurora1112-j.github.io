## 问题：对话会消失，思考不应该

重要的 AI 对话通常散落在 ChatGPT、Claude、Gemini 等不同平台里。平台保存了消息，却没有形成属于用户的连续记忆；当工具、账号或工作流改变时，过去的判断也很容易随之失去。

Vesti 把这个问题定义为一个“记忆层”问题，而不只是聊天记录导出问题。它尽量不改变用户原本的工具与标签页，只在浏览器中增加一个可搜索、可迁移的个人档案。

## 产品模型

Vesti 是两种彼此连接的工作空间：

- **浏览器扩展**：Threads、Insights、Data 与 Settings 靠近对话发生的地方，负责捕获、快速回看和系统控制
- **知识工作台**：Library、Explore 与 Network 负责阅读原始线程、形成笔记、追问档案并观察关系随时间出现

两种空间共享来源、线程与状态模型，但承担不同的信息密度。

Vesti 既是for human的，也是Agent-native的，希望它是你与Agent共同的知识库。


## 设计原则

### 不制造第二个工作流

记忆层应当贴近内容产生的地方。侧栏与覆盖层保持克制，让正在进行的聊天仍然是主界面。

### 本地优先是一种产品立场

默认把档案和索引保留在浏览器附近，减少对另一个云端账户的依赖。

你的知识资产属于你，Vesti不做又一个平台。

### AI 增强判断，而不是替代判断

自动整理的价值在于帮助用户回到自己的材料、比较过去的思路并发现联系，而不是制造一个看似确定的总结终点。

我们深知AI总结的高效，也警惕它的局限，因为无论这些summary多么事无巨细，它们也只是二手语料，真正宝贵的与激发思考的正在与你回溯的轨迹。

## 我负责的部分

产品定义、信息架构、交互与视觉设计，以及浏览器扩展和主要产品界面的全栈实现。案例覆盖从产品叙事、捕获流程与档案检索，到本地存储、演示页面和公共发布材料。

## 当前与未来形态

Vesti 已形成浏览器扩展、对话档案、搜索与探索界面，以及一套跨界面和印刷媒介的视觉系统。

在更广的语境里，考虑现在/未来的方向，我们还将探索怎么呈现思维的发散性（思维图谱可不够），怎么管理跨 session 的对话记录（不要简单拼接），怎么让 Agent 读取知识库（存储与呈现方式/交付物是否 Agent-native），等等。

<IntroParagraph>Vesti keeps the work people do with AI alive after a tab closes: prompts, dead ends, references, judgements, and decisions remain available for retrieval.</IntroParagraph>

## Problem: conversations disappear, thought should not

Important AI conversations are scattered across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other platforms. Platforms retain messages, but rarely create a continuous memory owned by the user. When tools, accounts, or workflows change, past judgement is easily lost with them.

Vesti frames this as a memory-layer problem rather than a transcript-export problem. It preserves the user's existing tools and tabs, adding a searchable and portable personal archive inside the browser.

## Product model

Vesti is organised as two connected working spaces:

- **Browser extension**: Threads, Insights, Data, and Settings stay close to where conversations happen, handling capture, quick return, and system control
- **Knowledge workspace**: Library, Explore, and Network support source reading, note-making, archive questions, and relationships unfolding over time

The two spaces share provenance, thread, and state models while carrying different densities.

Vesti is for humans, but it is also Agent-native. I hope it can become a knowledge base shared by you and your agents.

## Design principles

### Do not create a second workflow

Memory should remain close to where it is made. Restrained side panels and overlays preserve the active conversation as the primary interface.

### Local-first is a product position

Keeping archives and indexes near the browser reduces dependence on yet another cloud account.

Your knowledge assets belong to you. Vesti is not trying to become yet another platform.

### AI augments judgement

Automatic organisation should help people return to their own material, compare earlier thinking, and notice relationships—not manufacture a falsely definitive endpoint.

We know how efficient AI summaries can be, and we are wary of their limits. However exhaustive they become, they are still second-hand material. What is truly valuable, and what provokes new thought, is the trail you retrace through the original conversation.

## My role

Product definition, information architecture, interaction and visual design, plus full-stack implementation of the browser extension and core product surfaces. The work spans capture, archive retrieval, local storage, public narrative, and launch materials.

## Current and future form

Vesti now includes a browser extension, conversation archive, search and exploration surfaces, and a visual system shared across interface and print.

Looking further ahead, we still want to explore how to represent the divergent nature of thought (a thought graph may not be enough), how to manage conversations across sessions without simply stitching them together, and how agents should read the knowledge base (including whether its storage, presentation, and outputs are truly Agent-native).

Your feedback and collaboration are welcome.
