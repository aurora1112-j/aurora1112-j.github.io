<LanguageBlock lang="zh">

当 AI 直接给出答案时，用户失去了经过知识空间的路径。也许检索界面不该只返回结果，还应该让人看见它穿过了哪些主题、来源与旧想法。
</LanguageBlock>

<LanguageBlock lang="en">
When AI jumps directly to an answer, people lose the path through the knowledge space. A retrieval interface might show not only the result, but the topics, sources, and earlier ideas it travelled through.
</LanguageBlock>
