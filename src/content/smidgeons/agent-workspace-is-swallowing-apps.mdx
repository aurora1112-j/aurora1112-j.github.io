今天突然意识到一件有趣的事：Codex 这样的 AI 应用，最后可能会吞掉网站和 Electron 应用。我只需要在左侧与 Agent 对话，让它调用 Figma MCP 之类的工具，再在右侧检查结果。我几乎不需要打开另一个应用。

这意味着应用未必会消失，但它们可能不再是用户主动前往的“地方”，而会变成 Agent 在背后调用的能力。
</LanguageBlock>

<LanguageBlock lang="en">
Had a funny realization today: AI apps like Codex might end up swallowing websites and Electron apps. I can just talk to the agent on the left, let it use tools like Figma MCP, and check the result on the right. I barely need to open another app.

Apps may not disappear, but they may stop being destinations people deliberately visit and become capabilities an agent invokes behind the workspace.
</LanguageBlock>
