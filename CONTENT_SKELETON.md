# Botanical Systems content skeleton

This repository contains a compact bilingual content set for Chenxi Huang's personal knowledge garden.

Current themes:

- Artificial intelligence
- AI agents and human–AI collaboration
- Knowledge management and digital gardening
- Trust, provenance, memory, and context engineering

Collection sizes are intentionally limited to two or three entries. Placeholder URLs use `example.com` and should be replaced before publication.

Chinese and English titles, descriptions, and bodies are authored separately. The global language switch displays one language at a time.
